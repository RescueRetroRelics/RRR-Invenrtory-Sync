3 ⚡ Menu and footer disagree 1 on 1 page(s) high impact easy verified

Why it matters: Same link name going to two different places confuses shoppers and splits your SEO.

How to fix: Online Store > Navigation: open the main menu and the footer menu and point both items at the same page. For policies, use the built-in Shopify policy pages (Settings > Policies).

5 ⚡ Image has no alt text 3 on 2 page(s) medium impact easy verified

Why it matters: Screen readers skip it and Google Images can't index it. Alt text is free SEO for collectibles.

How to fix: Products: click the image in admin > 'Add alt text'. Pages/theme: Online Store > Customize > the image block > 'Image alt text'. Describe it like you'd tell a friend: 'Vintage 1965 Snoopy Hallmark ornament, red doghouse'.

How to fix: Admin > 'Search engine listing' > Edit > write a complete sentence or two, 120-155 characters.
