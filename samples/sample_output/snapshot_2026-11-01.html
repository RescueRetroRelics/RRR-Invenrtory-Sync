How to read this

  • Sales data covers Aug 25, 2026 to Nov 01, 2026.
  • Sales = item prices x quantity after item discounts. Shipping, tax and fees are not included.
  • Cancelled, voided and fully refunded orders are left out. Partial refunds are not subtracted.
  • '4-week avg' is the average of the 4 weeks before this one.
  • Every number here comes straight from your export files (verified). Slow movers are a flag to look at, not a verdict (hypothesis).
  • Slow mover = active, in stock, and no sale on Shopify or eBay in the last 60 days.
  • Shopify's product export has no 'date listed', so a brand-new item can show as slow. Add the eBay active-listings export to see how long each one has been listed.
