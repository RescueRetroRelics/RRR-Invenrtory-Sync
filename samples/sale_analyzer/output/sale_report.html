Notes on the data

  • 'Days to sell' is counted from the day the product was added to Shopify. Items listed on eBay before they were added to Shopify really took longer than shown, and sales from before they were on Shopify have no days-to-sell at all.
  • eBay order prices can include shipping, so eBay revenue is approximate. Sell-through and days-to-sell use unit counts and dates only, so they're not affected.
