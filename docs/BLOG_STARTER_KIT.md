# RRR blog starter kit

## Why the blog matters (the thing you were trying to remember)
Most RRR sales come from **Google search**. Product pages only show up when someone searches for that exact
item. Blog posts catch the searches *around* your products: questions, comparisons, gift ideas and
"full series list" lookups. Each post links straight to the matching collection, so a reader becomes a
shopper. Rescue stories also get shared on Facebook and Pinterest, which brings in people who've never heard
of you. Google also ranks sites higher when they're regularly updated with real, first-hand writing, and
nobody else can write your crew's stories.

**Rhythm:** one post every week or two beats ten posts in one weekend. Short is fine at 300–600 words.
Done > perfect.

## How the theme helps each post
- **Tag a post with a collection's handle** (e.g. `betty-boop`, `bundles`) and the bottom of the post
  automatically shows 4 products from that collection under "Shop this story." That's the traffic → sales
  bridge.
- Tags also become topic filter buttons on the blog page (e.g. *Collector Guides*, *Rescue Stories*, *Crew News*).
- Every post gets Google's Article markup, reading time, share buttons (Facebook, Pinterest, X), the Detroit
  rescue mission box and previous/next links automatically.
- **Featured image:** add one to every post. It shows on the blog page and when people share the link.

## Draft 1: Savannah Files a Formal Grievance
*Tags: Crew News, Savannah · Featured image: Savannah with her paw over her nose*

Every business needs a Head of Quality Control. Ours is Savannah Bananas, and this week she filed a grievance.

**The incident.** After a software update, a few of our listings briefly called Casey "he." Casey is a girl.
Casey received apology snacks. Extra ones.

**The grievance.** Savannah was not misgendered. Savannah was not wronged. Savannah received no snacks. In her
view, being *not* wronged while someone else gets compensated is its own kind of injustice. She filed her
complaint the way she files all complaints: by lying on the couch with one paw over her face and refusing to
make eye contact with management.

**The ruling.** Management has reviewed the case. Savannah has been awarded one (1) snack for emotional
distress and one (1) official photo on the Meet the Crew page, captioned for the record.

She has several more grievances pending. We'll keep you posted.

*Every order from Rescue Retro Relics helps fund Detroit dog rescue, which is how Savannah found us in the
first place. [Meet the whole crew →](/pages/meet-the-team)*
